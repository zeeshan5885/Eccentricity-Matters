Response to Major Concern.

1. In this work, we adopt semianalytic estimates for how well eccentricity can be measured. We have at no point
  specified the reference frequency used to characterize eccentricity.  Rather, the reference frequency used was chosen
  to calibrate an estimate of the chirp mass uncertainty, ignoring correlations.  Within the context of our simplified likelihood
  model for each event, we don't need to evolve any parameters with frequency.

2. We have added a paragraph at the end of section III-A to explain our likelihood model is motivated by the inspiral-dominated regime.
